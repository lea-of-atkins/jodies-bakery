Our Philosophy

Back to Real Food & Slow Craft

Wild Yeast & Time

Our sourdough starts with a bubbly, live wild culture nurtured daily. We believe in long cold-fermentations that break down gluten naturally and build deep, authentic flavor without shortcuts.

Easier Digestion

Whole Ingredients Only

No mystery additives, dough conditioners, or preservatives ever enter our home kitchen. Just stoneground flour, pure water, unrefined sea salt, real cultured butter, raw honey, and wholesome nuts and seeds.

Clean Nutrition

Mon–Thu Rhythm

We bake fresh in small batches Monday through Thursday. By Thursday afternoon, we pack up the campervan for weekend adventures, keeping our bake schedule personal and perfectly balanced.

Meet the Baker

Baked at home in Dudley, with passion & purpose.

Hi, I'm Jodie! Ever since we shifted toward a clean whole-food lifestyle, I wanted bread and bakes that not only tasted incredible but made us feel great. What started as weekend experiments in our Dudley kitchen for family and gym mates has gently blossomed into this micro-bakery.

Every loaf of sourdough is hand-shaped, every jar of granola is slow-roasted, and every brioche is baked with uncompromised care. Thank you for supporting our small-scale scratch baking!
